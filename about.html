About The 4 Gadys

Four besties, one dream — serving the best jagung susu on campus.

Who We Are

The 4 Gadys is a student business from TVET MARA Lumut. We're four students running this as part of our Digital Business subject — no big budget, just good ingredients and a lot of trial and error.

Our product is Jagung Susu Creamy — sweet corn cooked in milk, butter, and our signature creamy sauce. Simple, satisfying, and made for students.

Meet The Team

Our Mission & Vision

Mission: To serve delicious, high-quality Jagung Susu to fellow students using fresh ingredients and easy online ordering.

Vision: To become a trusted, well-loved snack brand among students — and beyond.
