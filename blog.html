What Makes Our Jagung Susu Different

It's not just boiled corn and milk — we make our own creamy sauce from scratch to get that perfect balance of sweet.

Most versions you'll find use an instant syrup base, but we wanted ours to taste homemade. Our sauce is made fresh in small batches, which is part of why we can only make a limited amount each day — but it's worth it.

How to Enjoy Your Jagung Susu at Its Best

Best enjoyed cold or slightly warm right after it arrives. Here's how to store leftovers without ruining the texture.

If you're not drinking it right away, keep it chilled and finish it within a few hours for the best texture. We don't recommend microwaving it — that can break the creamy sauce. Give it a quick stir before drinking to bring it back together.
